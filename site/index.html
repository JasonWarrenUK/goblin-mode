How the Two Connect

/dossier-record writes facts about a real person. /red-sabotage turns a person into a nine-field model of how they review a document.

When a persona slug matches a dossier file, red-sabotage derives what it can from the recorded facts and interviews for the rest, then writes the result to the persona store. Nothing flows back the other way, because a prediction about someone's temperament must never end up filed as a fact about them.
